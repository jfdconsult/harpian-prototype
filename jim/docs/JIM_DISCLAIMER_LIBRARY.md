# JIM Disclaimer Library
## Pre-Approved Regulatory Disclosures

**Version:** 1.0 | **Classification:** Confidential — Internal Use Only  
**Note:** Any modification to this document requires Compliance Admin approval and version increment before deployment.

---

## Usage Rules

- All disclaimers are keyed by DISC-ID and stored in `config/disclaimer_library.json`
- JIM selects disclaimers automatically based on intent_id and blocks used
- No disclaimer may be modified inline by JIM
- New disclaimers require Compliance Admin approval before addition
- DISC-002 must appear **before** any simulation table — never after
- DISC-006 must appear **in full** for all ACTION_REQUEST responses — never abbreviated

## Full Disclaimer Texts

**DISC-001 — Historical Performance**
> Past performance does not guarantee future results. Returns shown are historical and may not be indicative of future performance. Investing involves risk, including possible loss of principal.

**DISC-002 — Simulation / Hypothetical Performance**
> This is a hypothetical simulation generated by the HARPIAN HRD Engine. Simulated results do not represent actual trading, are not guaranteed, and may differ materially from actual results. Hypothetical performance has inherent limitations and does not account for market impact, taxes, transaction costs, or behavioral factors. This simulation does not constitute a recommendation or guarantee of future performance.

**DISC-003 — Risk Number**
> Risk Number is a proprietary HARPIAN metric based on historical volatility and portfolio construction factors. It is a diagnostic tool, not a predictor of future performance or a guarantee of loss protection. Risk tolerance targets are set by the client and advisor through the formal suitability process.

**DISC-004 — StormGuard**
> StormGuard is a systematic, rules-based regime detection model. It does not guarantee protection from loss in any market condition. Historical regime detection accuracy is not indicative of future performance. StormGuard outputs are informational signals for the advisor's review, not trading instructions.

**DISC-005 — AlphaDroid**
> AlphaDroid is a momentum-based systematic model. Model signals are quantitative outputs based on historical data and are not recommendations to buy or sell any security. Past signal accuracy does not guarantee future performance. All AlphaDroid outputs should be evaluated by a licensed advisor within the context of the client's full portfolio and investment policy.

**DISC-006 — Suitability / Advisor Escalation**
> Investment suitability determinations require the formal best-interest analysis performed by a licensed financial advisor under applicable regulations including SEC Regulation Best Interest. JIM is a diagnostic and educational tool; it does not perform suitability analysis and does not constitute investment advice. All investment decisions should be made with a licensed advisor.

**DISC-007 — Alignment Score**
> Alignment Score is a proprietary HARPIAN diagnostic metric. It reflects the degree of mathematical alignment between portfolio characteristics and the client's stated objectives, risk tolerance, and time horizon. It is not a guarantee of suitability, not a compliance determination, and not a substitute for the formal suitability process.

**DISC-008 — Form CRS / Relationship Disclosure**
> HARPIAN is a portfolio engineering and analytical platform. It is not a registered investment advisor. Your advisor's Form CRS describes the nature of your advisory relationship, services provided, fees, and conflicts of interest. A copy of your advisor's Form CRS is available from your advisor upon request.

**DISC-009 — Stress Test / Scenario Analysis**
> Stress test and scenario analysis outputs are hypothetical simulations based on historical events or defined parameters. They do not represent actual performance and are not predictions of future events. Market conditions in future stress scenarios may differ materially from historical analogs. This analysis is for informational purposes only and does not constitute a recommendation.

**DISC-010 — External / Social Media Content**
> This content has not yet received compliance approval for external distribution or publication. Do not share, distribute, or publish this material externally until it has been reviewed and approved by a designated compliance supervisor. Unauthorized distribution may violate applicable securities laws and FINRA rules.

**DISC-011 — Incomplete Data**
> This analysis is based on the data currently loaded in the HARPIAN terminal for this client. JIM does not have access to data outside the current context. Results may be incomplete if not all relevant accounts, positions, or client information are loaded.

**DISC-012 — Low Confidence**
> JIM's confidence in this response is below the standard threshold. The data available may be insufficient for a complete analysis. This response should be treated as preliminary and reviewed by the advisor of record before any action is taken.

**DISC-013 — Projection / Estimate**
> Projected figures are mathematical estimates based on current portfolio data and stated assumptions. They are not guarantees of future performance. Actual outcomes will depend on market conditions, client behavior, advisor decisions, and other factors that cannot be predicted.

**DISC-014 — Benchmark Comparison**
> Benchmark comparisons are shown for illustrative purposes only. The portfolio's investment objectives, strategy, and composition may differ materially from the benchmark. Benchmark returns do not reflect fees, expenses, or the impact of taxes. It is not possible to invest directly in an index.
