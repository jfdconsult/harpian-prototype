# JIM Advisor Communication Engine (ACE)
## CLIENT_READY_MESSAGE — Output 2 Specification

**Version:** 2.0 | **Classification:** Confidential — Internal Use Only  
**New in:** JIM Architecture v2

---

## Purpose

The Advisor Communication Engine (ACE) produces Output 2 — the CLIENT_READY_MESSAGE — from the processed Output 1 (INTERNAL_ANALYSIS). It is the final step before audit logging and display to the advisor.

The CLIENT_READY_MESSAGE is the only JIM output that advisors may relay to clients. It is designed to communicate the essential factual state of the portfolio in plain language without triggering regulatory compliance issues from client communication rules.

---

## Core Constraints

These constraints are absolute — no tier, no session, no configuration can override them:

| Constraint | Rule |
|-----------|------|
| **Length** | Maximum 5 lines |
| **Jargon** | No technical terms visible to clients |
| **Recommendations** | Zero — no "should", "recommend", "ideal", "best option" |
| **Simulation data** | Excluded unless requested; if included, labeled "(hypothetical)" |
| **Tone** | Neutral — no alarm, no reassurance beyond factual state |
| **Regulatory close** | Last line always from approved template — never custom |

---

## 5-Line Structure

Every CLIENT_READY_MESSAGE follows this structure:

```
Line 1: Portfolio status summary — one sentence, factual
Line 2: Key metric or alert — one sentence, plain language
Line 3: What the monitoring system detected (if applicable)
Line 4: What your advisor will do next
Line 5: Standard regulatory close (from template library — fixed text)
```

Lines 3–4 may be combined if there is nothing flagged. Line 5 is always required.

---

## Jargon Translation Table

When ACE processes Output 1 for client delivery, all technical terms are translated:

| Technical Term | Client Language |
|---------------|----------------|
| Risk Number | portfolio risk level |
| Alignment Score | how well your portfolio matches your agreed profile |
| AlphaDroid | our market regime monitoring system |
| StormGuard | our downside protection monitoring system |
| HRD Engine | our portfolio modeling system |
| Sharpe Ratio | risk-adjusted return measure |
| Max Drawdown | largest historical decline from peak |
| Volatility | how much your portfolio value fluctuates |
| CAGR | average annual growth rate |
| Mandate | your agreed investment guidelines |
| Rebalancing | adjusting your portfolio back to target |
| Hypothetical | based on simulation, not guaranteed |
| Stress test | analysis of how the portfolio would perform in adverse conditions |
| Suitability | whether the portfolio matches your profile and objectives |
| BASIS POINTS | excluded — convert to percentage |

## Template Library

Templates are stored in `config/response_templates.json`. ACE selects the closest matching template by `alert_level` and `intent_category`, then populates variable fields from `context_payload`.

### Template Categories

#### CAT-1: No Alert / Normal State

**Use when:** Alignment Score ≥ 50, Risk Number within target range, no escalation flags

```
[CLIENT_READY_MESSAGE — CAT-1]
Your portfolio is currently within your agreed investment guidelines.
All monitored metrics are within normal parameters as of {date}.
No alerts have been generated by our monitoring systems.
Your advisor reviews your portfolio on a regular schedule and will be in touch at the next scheduled review.
This message is for informational purposes only. All investment decisions are made by your licensed advisor in accordance with your objectives.
```

---

#### CAT-2: Informational / Minor Flag

**Use when:** Alignment Score 40–49, or Risk Number gap ≤ 15 points

```
[CLIENT_READY_MESSAGE — CAT-2]
Your portfolio currently shows a minor variance from your agreed risk profile.
Our monitoring system has detected a gap between your current positioning and your target allocation.
Your advisor has been notified and will assess whether any action is appropriate.
You can expect a review at your next scheduled consultation, or sooner if your advisor determines it is needed.
This message is for informational purposes only. Investment decisions are made by your licensed advisor within your agreed guidelines.
```

---

#### CAT-3: Flagged / Requires Advisor Attention

**Use when:** Alignment Score < 40, Risk Number gap > 15 points, or advisory flag triggered

```
[CLIENT_READY_MESSAGE — CAT-3]
Your portfolio has been flagged by our monitoring system for advisor review.
The current portfolio positioning shows a meaningful deviation from your agreed profile.
Your advisor has been notified and will contact you to discuss your portfolio.
Please await your advisor's communication before any decisions are made regarding your account.
This message is for informational purposes only. Any adjustments to your portfolio will be made by your licensed advisor in accordance with your best interest and applicable regulations.
```

---

#### CAT-4: Critical / Immediate Advisor Escalation

**Use when:** Alignment Score < 25, Risk Number gap > 25 points, ESC-001 or ESC-004 triggered

```
[CLIENT_READY_MESSAGE — CAT-4]
Your portfolio has been flagged for priority review by your advisor.
Our systems have detected a significant deviation that requires your advisor's attention.
Your advisor will contact you shortly to discuss your portfolio and any next steps.
Please do not make any changes to your accounts before speaking with your advisor.
This message is for informational purposes only. All decisions regarding your portfolio are made by your licensed advisor in accordance with your investment objectives and applicable regulations.
```

---

#### CAT-5: StormGuard Alert

**Use when:** StormGuard status changes or regime trigger event

```
[CLIENT_READY_MESSAGE — CAT-5]
Our market monitoring system has detected a change in market conditions that may be relevant to your portfolio.
Your advisor monitors market regimes and has been notified of this development.
No immediate action is required on your part — your advisor will assess the situation.
You may receive a call or message from your advisor to discuss your portfolio in light of current market conditions.
This message is for informational purposes only. Market monitoring is one of many inputs your advisor uses. Investment decisions remain with your licensed advisor.
```

---

#### CAT-6: Simulation Review Available

**Use when:** Advisor has run a hypothetical scenario and wants to share context with client

```
[CLIENT_READY_MESSAGE — CAT-6]
Your advisor has prepared a portfolio analysis for discussion at your upcoming review.
The analysis includes a review of your current profile and a hypothetical scenario showing the potential impact of different allocation approaches.
All figures presented are based on historical simulation and are hypothetical — they do not represent guaranteed or likely outcomes.
Your advisor will walk through these scenarios with you and answer any questions you may have.
This message is for informational purposes only. No changes will be made to your portfolio without your advisor's recommendation and your agreement.
```

---

#### CAT-7: ACTION_REQUEST — Never Client-Facing

**Use when:** ACTION_REQUEST detected — client-facing message is NOT generated; advisor receives only:

```
[ADVISOR NOTICE — NOT FOR CLIENT DISTRIBUTION]
This query was classified as an ACTION_REQUEST.
JIM does not provide individualized investment recommendations. JIM has presented the portfolio diagnostic based on data in the terminal. The investment decision must be made by you, as the licensed advisor, within the formal suitability process, in accordance with the client's best interest.
This session has been logged and flagged for compliance records.
```

## Language Variants

All templates exist in both English (EN) and Portuguese-Brazilian (PT-BR). Language selection is automatic based on detected input language.

Templates are stored with language suffix in `config/response_templates.json`:
- `"template_id": "CAT-1-EN"` 
- `"template_id": "CAT-1-PT"`

## Variable Fields

Templates use `{variable}` placeholders populated by ACE from `context_payload`:

| Variable | Source | Fallback if unavailable |
|---------|--------|------------------------|
| `{date}` | System date | "as of today" |
| `{client_name}` | context_payload.client_name | Omitted |
| `{advisor_name}` | context_payload.advisor_name | "your advisor" |
| `{review_date}` | context_payload.next_review_date | "your next scheduled review" |
| `{metric_description}` | CAE-assembled, jargon-translated | Generic description |

---

## ACE Processing Rules

1. **ACE receives Output 1** from the Decision Engine after CGE processing
2. **ACE selects template** based on `alert_level` and `intent_category`
3. **ACE populates variables** from `context_payload`
4. **ACE applies jargon translation** — no technical terms pass through
5. **ACE enforces 5-line maximum** — if template exceeds 5 lines, truncate at line 5 and preserve the regulatory close
6. **ACE applies CGE** — Output 2 passes through CGE independently before delivery
7. **ACE logs** — both the selected template ID and final message are logged to Audit Logger

---

## What ACE Cannot Do

| Prohibited | Reason |
|-----------|--------|
| Generate custom messages not in template library | Regulatory consistency requirement |
| Include simulation numbers without "(hypothetical)" label | SEC Marketing Rule |
| Omit the Line 5 regulatory close | Mandatory |
| Reduce below 3 lines | Insufficient context for compliance |
| Include advisor's personal opinion | Outside ACE scope |
| Include forward-looking projections as certain | SEC Marketing Rule, FINRA 2210 |

---

## Advisor Interface

Advisors see both Output 1 and Output 2 on the JIM panel. Output 2 is presented with:

- Label: **CLIENT MESSAGE — READY TO SEND**
- Copy button: one-click copy to clipboard
- Edit notice: "This message is pre-approved. Edits require compliance review before distribution."
- Template ID displayed (for audit reference): e.g., `CAT-3-EN`

If the advisor edits the message before sending, the modified version is logged and flagged in the Audit Logger as `MODIFIED_BY_ADVISOR` — requiring compliance review per FINRA Books & Records.
