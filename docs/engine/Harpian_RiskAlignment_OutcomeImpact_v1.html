C·01Cost of Inaction — What Staying Still Actually Means
Inaction is also a decision. This is its cost.
By not adjusting your allocation, you are currently sacrificing ~22 percentage points of goal success probability and ~4 additional years of required time horizon. This is not a projection — it is the measured difference between your current path and the recommended one.
Probability sacrificed
−22%
P(Goal) at current allocation vs adjusted: 52% vs 74%. Each year of delay compounds this gap as compounding opportunities are foregone.
Additional time required
+4 yrs
At current pace, target is reached in year 11. Adjusted allocation reaches it in year 7. The 4-year gap is the cost of inaction in time units.
Expected shortfall (P50)
−$840K
Median terminal wealth at year 12 with current allocation vs adjusted. The gap is not statistical abstraction — it is deferred capital.
Cost of Inaction is computed as: (P_goal_adjusted − P_goal_current) and (Time_adjusted − Time_current) and (Terminal_wealth_adjusted_P50 − Terminal_wealth_current_P50). Never framed as a threat — framed as a measurement. The numbers do the work.

D·02System Language — Institutional Tone with Logical Urgency
The system creates urgency through logic — not emotion. The numbers do the work. The language is precise, concise, never hyperbolic, never retail.
Instead ofThe system saysWhy
❌ "Your portfolio is at risk!" ✓ "Your portfolio exceeds your risk tolerance by 13 points." Numbers create fact. Exclamation marks create noise.
❌ "Don't miss this opportunity!" ✓ "Inaction currently costs ~22% of goal success probability." Measured cost creates genuine urgency.
❌ "Maximize your returns!" ✓ "This adjustment improves expected return from 6–9% to 9–13% annually." Specificity replaces persuasion.
❌ "Smart investors choose Core 22." ✓ "Core 22 is indicated when portfolio is under-risked and Risk Budget is available." Logic replaces appeal to identity.
❌ "Act now before it's too late." ✓ "Each year of delay at current pace extends time to target by approximately 0.4 years." Compounding math creates its own urgency.
