BLOCK C Asymmetry Panel — Risk/Reward Structure
C·01Upside / Downside / Asymmetry Ratio
The asymmetry ratio is the single most important structural insight. It answers: for every unit of risk taken, how many units of potential return exist? A ratio above 3x is considered institutionally acceptable. Below 1.5x = unfavorable structure.

Asymmetry ratio = (P75 return − P50 return) ÷ (P50 return − P10 return). Values above 3× indicate the distribution is positively skewed — more outcome mass on the upside. StormGuard Armor in Core 11 mechanically improves this ratio by truncating the left tail.

BLOCK E Final Decision Block — Complete Closing Screen
E·01Goal Feasibility Label — Always Visible, Unambiguous
Feasible
P(Goal) ≥ 75% with suggested allocation. Goal achievable within stated horizon and risk parameters.
Borderline
P(Goal) 50–74%. Achievable but requires adjustment. One variable change resolves it.
Not Feasible
P(Goal) <50% even at max tolerance. Requires: ↑ risk OR ↑ time OR ↓ target.
Exactly one label visible at all times. Label recolors the top banner of the entire final screen — not just a badge. At "Not Feasible" state, the three levers (from Outcome Impact doc) appear inline below the label with exact numbers.
